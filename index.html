What You'll Get

A few things we're building into the ATLXS experience

Meet Gamers

Connect with players who share your games, your vibe, and your energy — outside the lobby.

Real Conversations

Chat, share clips, and build friendships that last longer than one match.

Game-First

Built by a gamer, for gamers. No noise — just the things that actually matter to you.

Safe Space

A community that respects the culture and keeps the vibes clean.

TRADING AT IT'S BEST

The ATLXS app enables it's users to buy and sell items available only within the app. After owning items, users can trade them with other players or sell them on the marketplace where they get paid real money.

HACKS

Access to tons of mods and hacks of your favorite games — yes even your favorite Garena, Netease, and Riot games Games 😉. But not for free, as users would have to purchase the in-app-currency to access them.
